# Privacy Policy — Orb Flip Dash

**Last updated:** 2026-09-07

This privacy policy applies to the Orb Flip Dash Android application ("App") developed and operated by Superora Studio.

## Information We Collect
Orb Flip Dash does not collect, store, or share personal information directly.

## Third-Party Advertising
This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:
- Device identifiers (e.g., Android advertising ID)
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- Usage data (ad impressions, clicks, interactions)

AdMob's data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Retention
Advertising data collected by AdMob is retained for ad-serving purposes only. No personal data is stored on our servers. Data is automatically deleted within 6 months of app uninstallation.

## Account Deletion
This app does not require user accounts. If you wish to request deletion of any data associated with your device, contact superorastudio@gmail.com.

## Children's Privacy
Orb Flip Dash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

## Contact
